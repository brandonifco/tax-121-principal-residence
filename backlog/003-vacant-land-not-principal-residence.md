Evidence, verbatim:

```text
(3) Vacant land—(i) In general. The sale or exchange of vacant land is not a sale or exchange of the taxpayer's principal residence unless— (A) The vacant land is adjacent to land containing the dwelling unit of the taxpayer's principal residence; (B) The taxpayer owned and used the vacant land as part of the taxpayer's principal residence; (C) The taxpayer sells or exchanges the dwelling unit in a sale or exchange that meets the requirements of section 121 within 2 years before or 2 years after the date of the sale or exchange of the vacant land; and (D) The requirements of section 121 have otherwise been met with respect to the vacant land.
```

## Ambiguity

```text
Condition (A) requires the vacant land to be "adjacent to" land containing the dwelling unit, and the corpus fixes no measure of adjacency: land across a public road, land touching at a corner, and land separated by a strip the taxpayer does not own are each inside or outside the rule depending on a reading the corpus does not give. Nobody is named to decide.
```
